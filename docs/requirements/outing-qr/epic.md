# Outing QR code to join and vote

Every open outing (kèo) page in Drink Votes shows the outing's QR code below the outing
details, to anyone who opens it. Colleagues nearby scan it with their phone camera and land on
the outing's normal page, where they say Going and vote exactly as today. Only the code is
shown (no caption, link text, copy or download button), it is not shown anywhere else, and it
disappears once the outing is closed, waiting for the organizer's choice or cancelled.

Covers FR-01..FR-09 and NFR-01..NFR-05. No change to the voting, closing, tie-break or identity
rules of Drink Votes.

Details: `docs/requirements/outing-qr/prd.md`, `requirements.md` (signed off 2026-10-16 by Cong Dang).
